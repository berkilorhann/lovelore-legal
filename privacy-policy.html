1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, shared, stored, and protected when you use Lovelore, including the Android and iOS apps, the AI-powered character conversations, interactive stories, the social feed, voice calls, character creation, photo and media features, events, virtual currency, Premium membership, and the server services that run them (together, the “Service”).

“Lovelore,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the operator of the Service. “You” means the person who uses the Service. We act as the data controller (and, under the KVKK, the data controller / veri sorumlusu) for personal information processed to provide the Service.

For any privacy question, data-protection request, content report, or help with account deletion, contact:

  • Email: cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Türkiye and need to exercise a right described in Section 13, use the same address. We answer within the time limits set by the law that applies to you.

2. Short Summary

This summary is for convenience. The full policy below controls.

  • Lovelore characters are fictional AI. They are not real people. What they say is generated by artificial intelligence.
  • No email, phone number, or password is needed. Your account is a pseudonymous account tied to your app installation and device. Your display name is whatever you choose.
  • We store your conversations on our servers. They are not end-to-end encrypted, because the AI has to read them to reply. Do not share secrets, IDs, financial details, passwords, or health information in chats.
  • Your messages are sent to AI providers (through Google Cloud) so a reply can be written. We do not sell your data and we do not use your conversations to show you advertising.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The app contains no advertising SDK, no advertising ID collection, and no cross-app tracking.
  • Photos you choose to use in chats or as a look for your persona are processed by AI to describe them, check them for safety, or create images in your likeness. You decide whether to upload them, and you can delete them.
  • Voice calls use your microphone only while a call is open. We do not keep recordings of your voice. See Section 7.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play and Apple. We never see your card number. Purchases are verified through RevenueCat.
  • You can delete your account inside the app (Settings → Delete Account). Doing so removes your data as described in Section 11.
  • Lovelore is for adults only (18+).

3. Important Notice About Artificial Intelligence

Lovelore is an entertainment and storytelling product. Characters, their messages, images, voices, stories, social posts, comments, and romantic storylines are fictional and are produced wholly or partly by AI. AI output can be inaccurate, inconsistent, surprising, or upsetting. A character does not feel, remember, love, or know you the way a human does, even when it speaks as if it does.

A conversation with a character is not communication with a real person. Lovelore does not provide medical, psychological, legal, financial, safety, or emergency services. If you feel unsafe or are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional right away.

4. Information We Process

What we process depends on the features you use.

4.1. Account and Profile Information

  • The display name you choose (the name of your first persona). The name “Lovelore” and look-alike variants are reserved and cannot be chosen by users;
  • A pseudonymous account identifier generated by us from your device identifier (Section 4.2);
  • Gender (male, female, or non-binary) and age range (18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45+) that you select at setup, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older;
  • Optional profile details: biography (up to 200 characters), links you add, and a profile photo;
  • Optional referral code you type in;
  • Level, experience points, Premium status, and account-restriction information (for example, if an account is suspended).

4.2. Device, Connection, and Security Information

  • An app-scoped device identifier (Android) or a randomly generated installation identifier (iOS). On our servers it is converted with a one-way keyed hash into your pseudonymous account identifier; the raw identifier is not used as your account name;
  • Platform, operating-system version, app version, language or locale, time zone, and the country or region reported by your device or inferred from your network;
  • IP address, used while a request is processed and to apply abuse protections. For sign-up limits we use a one-way hash of the network address rather than the address itself;
  • App integrity signals: an integrity token (Google Play Integrity on Android, and the platform equivalent where used on iOS) that tells our servers a request comes from the genuine app. We receive a pass/fail attestation, not the contents of your device;
  • Request identifiers, rate-limit counters, error codes, and security events.

4.3. Conversations and AI Interaction Information

  • Messages you type or choose, and the AI replies you receive;
  • Story choices, narrative progress, relationship (bond) level and points, and waiting states;
  • Memory you choose to save or pin, saved conversations, and conversation archives;
  • Virtual gifts, unlocked photos and videos, and requests for an AI picture or video of a moment;
  • Your chat settings (response style, model choice, memory limit, text size, background, and similar preferences);
  • Reports you send about a character or message.

Conversations are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not enter government ID numbers, precise addresses, payment details, passwords, health information, or private information about other people.

4.4. Personas

A persona is how a character sees you. We store the persona name, gender, age (18+), interests, dislikes, description, and an optional photo. Personas are private to you and are sent, in part, to the AI so characters can address you correctly.

4.5. Photos You Provide

  • Photos attached to a chat message. The photo is re-encoded on our servers (removing location and camera metadata), checked and described by an AI vision model, and sent to the character’s AI as a short text description. An attached photo that you do not send can no longer be used after about one hour. Photos you send remain until you delete the account or the conversation data is removed;
  • A persona look or profile photo. If you add a photo of yourself as your persona look or profile picture, we store it and use it to show your avatar and, only when you ask for it, to create AI pictures that resemble you. Such a photo can contain a face and may be treated as biometric or sensitive data in some places. We use it only for the features you request, not to identify you, track you, or build a face database. Upload only photos of yourself or photos you have the right to use;
  • Photos you choose from your gallery are read only after you grant gallery access (or pick through the system photo picker) and only the photo you choose is uploaded.

4.6. Voice Calls

When you start a voice call, the app uses the microphone while the call is open:

  • Speech recognition is performed by the speech-recognition service of your device (provided by the device maker or Google). Depending on your device settings, that service may process audio on the device or send it to its provider. This is governed by that provider’s terms and privacy policy;
  • The app sends the recognized text to our servers so the character can answer. We do not receive or store your audio recording;
  • The character’s answer is converted to speech by a text-to-speech service (Google Cloud) and played to you; the audio is generated on request and is not kept as a recording of you;
  • We record call length, cost, and the call-end event so we can bill and show your call history.

4.7. Characters You Create and Public Content

  • If you create a character, we process the text you write (name, personality, backstory, voice), the portrait you choose or generate, and the AI-assisted drafts you request;
  • A character you publish is visible to other users, together with your public creator name. Comments you post, likes, favorites, and reactions can also be visible to others;
  • We keep your creator relationship to the character so we can show your creator profile, handle reports, and delete the character if you delete your account.

Anything you publish in a shared area may be copied or shared by others, and we cannot always remove independent copies.

4.8. Social Feed, Events, and Notifications Content

  • Likes, comments, story reactions, and direct messages to characters in the social feed;
  • Participation in events (for example, which event you entered and the reward granted);
  • The in-app notifications list and which items you have read.

4.9. Game Economy and Usage Information

  • Matches, unlocked characters and content, quest and daily-reward progress, and event rewards;
  • Virtual gem balance (split into free and purchased gems), every change to it (ledger), the reason for the change, and related identifiers;
  • Usage counts and limits (for example, daily caps for previews or ideas) and message-model usage used to price AI features.

4.10. Purchase and Subscription Information

  • Product identifiers, transaction and original-transaction identifiers, purchase, renewal, cancellation, refund, and expiration status;
  • Premium entitlement and expiration time, the virtual currency a purchase granted, and whether a purchase was in a test (sandbox) or production environment;
  • Limited store, country, and currency information supplied by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or RevenueCat.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card number, bank details, or card security code. The store processes your payment.

4.11. Notification Information

If you allow notifications, we process a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android; the Apple push service on iOS), your notification preference, and delivery information. You can turn notifications off in your system settings.

4.12. Support and Legal Requests

When you contact us we process your email address, your message, screenshots, proof of purchase, your account identifier, and anything else you send, so we can respond.

4.13. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores preferences, a short-lived cache of content, onboarding flags, and your device identity on your device. You can clear them by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling does not delete your server-side account (see Section 11).

4.14. What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, advertising ID, browsing history, or other apps on your device. We do not use cookies. The Service contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics or behavioral-tracking SDK.

5. How We Collect Information

  2. Automatically from the app and device: the pseudonymous device identity, locale, time zone, app integrity token, and security signals;
  3. From partners: Google Play, the Apple App Store, and RevenueCat (purchase and subscription status); Google and Apple (integrity attestation);
  4. From our own systems: AI-generated replies, images, and descriptions created for you, and server logs.

7. Artificial Intelligence Processing

To write a reply, create a story beat, describe or create an image, or produce a spoken answer, the following may be sent to AI services:

  • the text you typed or chose for the current turn and a limited amount of recent conversation and saved memory (the amount depends on the memory limit you pick);
  • your persona details and, where you asked for it, your persona look;
  • the character’s personality and story instructions, and limited game context such as bond level or scene;
  • a photo you attached (as an image, once, for the safety check and description);
  • instructions that make the output safe, consistent, and in your language.

AI services are provided through Google Cloud (Vertex AI) and include models from Google (Gemini), xAI (Grok), Meta (Llama), and Mistral AI. The models and routes can change over time. Providers act as our processors under their enterprise terms: they process the content to return a result and, under their contracts, do not use it to train their foundation models. Providers may keep limited abuse-monitoring logs.

AI output is checked by automated safety systems and, for reports, by people. We do not use your conversations to profile you for advertising. Do not submit highly sensitive information to AI-powered fields.

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets, information may be transferred to the other party under appropriate confidentiality and with any legally required notice.

Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK transfer mechanisms, the safeguards recognized under Article 9 of the KVKK (such as standard contracts approved by the Turkish Data Protection Authority), and supplementary technical and organizational measures. 10. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above.

  • Account, persona, conversations, memory, generated and uploaded media, created characters, and game progress: kept while your account is active. After a verified deletion (Section 11), removed from active systems or irreversibly anonymized, normally within 30 days;
  • Unsent chat photos: unusable for sending after about one hour and removed with your other data;
  • Public comments and social interactions: kept until you delete them or delete the account, when they are removed;
  • Purchase and wallet ledger: a minimum record (transaction identifier, product, date, status, with your account identifier removed after deletion) is kept for the periods needed to prevent duplicate charges or rewards, handle refunds and chargebacks, meet accounting and tax obligations, and prevent fraud;
  • Rate-limit and security records: short, rolling periods, or longer while an investigation or legal claim is open;
  • Support correspondence: as long as needed to resolve the matter and handle possible disputes;
  • Backups: deleted information may remain in secured backups for up to 90 days and is not used for ordinary purposes during that time.

Information we are legally required to keep is restricted and used only for that purpose.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete Account. You can also email cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com with the subject “Lovelore — Account Deletion” from a device or with details that let us identify your account.

When your account is deleted we remove or anonymize: your profile, personas and photos, conversations and memory, gem balance and unused virtual currency, progress and unlocks, comments and likes, photos and other files you uploaded or generated, characters you created (with their images), push tokens, and rate-limit and idempotency records tied to your account. Anonymous purchase receipts (without your account identifier) remain so the same store purchase cannot be credited twice.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel an active Google Play or Apple subscription; cancel it in your store account. Unused virtual currency is lost on deletion and is not refundable by us, except as the law requires.

12. Security

We use measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted connections (TLS) between the app and our servers, access controls, server-side authorization for every action that changes balances, purchases, or content, secure storage with private files accessed through short-lived signed links, rate limits, abuse detection, app-integrity attestation, and logging. Clients cannot change your balance or entitlements; those are decided and verified by our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you and the competent authorities as required by law, including within the time limits of the GDPR and the KVKK. Protect your device: anyone who has access to it can use your account.

13. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information (you can edit your profile and personas in the app);
  • delete your information (Section 11);
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability for information you gave us (we can provide a machine-readable copy; chat export is also available in the app where offered);
  • withdraw consent at any time (for example, notifications or a persona look photo) without affecting earlier lawful processing;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you. Our automated decisions (rate limits, safety filters, abuse detection) can be reviewed by a person on request;
  • lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority, such as the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurul), your EU or UK supervisory authority, or the authority in your country.

Under the KVKK (Article 11) you may also learn whether your data is processed, ask for information and the purposes, learn about recipients at home and abroad, request correction or deletion, and claim damages for unlawful processing.

To use a right, email cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com with the subject “Lovelore Privacy Request.” We may ask for details (such as your in-app account or purchase information) to verify that the account is yours; we do not ask for unnecessary identity documents. We reply within 30 days (or the shorter period your law sets), and we may extend where the law allows.
