Delete Your Lovelore Account and Data

Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can delete your Lovelore account and the data tied to it at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lovelore.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the notice and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away from the app’s side, and your data is removed from our systems as described below.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app (for example, you uninstalled it or lost your phone), email us:

  • To: cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com
  • Subject: Lovelore — Account Deletion
  • Include: the name you used in Lovelore and, if you have it, a purchase receipt or order number. Lovelore accounts are tied to your device and have no email or password, so we may need details that help us identify your account. We will never ask for your card number or password.

We reply within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your profile, display name, and personas (including any photos)
  • Your conversations, saved memory, and conversation archives
  • Your virtual gem balance (unused gems are lost and are not refundable by us) and your game progress, unlocks, quests, and rewards
  • Comments, likes, and other activity in the social feed
  • Photos and other files you uploaded or that were generated for you
  • Characters you created, together with their images
  • Notification tokens and technical records tied to your account

What may be kept

  • An anonymous purchase receipt (store transaction ID, product, date — with your account identifier removed) so the same store purchase cannot be credited twice and to meet accounting and fraud-prevention obligations.
  • Records we must keep by law, which are restricted and used only for that purpose.
  • Deleted data may remain in secured backups for up to 90 days and is not used for anything else during that time.

Good to know

  • Deleting the app does not delete your account. Use one of the options above.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel Lovelore Premium in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions (or your Apple ID subscriptions) so you are not charged again.
  • Deletion cannot be undone.

For how we handle your data in general, see the Privacy Policy. Questions? Email cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com.
